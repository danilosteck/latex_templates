## Study object

<!-- One or two sentences. What exactly are we studying? -->
The use of large language model (LLM) based AI agents to support academic
literature research and bibliometric analysis.
## Core questions

<!-- The questions whose answers become the viewpoint map. 3–7 is a good range. -->
1. How are LLM-based agents applied to literature review and bibliometric work?
2. What are the main benefits claimed, and by whom?
3. What limitations, risks, or criticisms are raised?
4. Which evaluation methods and quality criteria are used?
## Inclusion / exclusion criteria

- **Include:** peer-reviewed articles, reviews, and conference papers; years 2020 onward.
- **Exclude:** non-academic sources, opinion pieces, predatory venues.
